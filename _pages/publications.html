Our research explores root–soil interactions, root architecture, soil sensing and plant adaptation to soil physical and chemical stresses.

View our publications on Google Scholar →

Recent Research Highlights

Root hair responses to soil compaction

Soil compaction alters root hair development and spatial organisation, revealing how roots respond to changes in their physical environment.

Root anatomical responses to soil compaction

Compacted soil induces radial expansion of root cortical cells, demonstrating a structural response to the soil physical environment.

Soil structure and ethylene diffusion

Three-dimensional soil imaging reveals how soil pore structure can influence the movement and diffusion of ethylene around roots.
